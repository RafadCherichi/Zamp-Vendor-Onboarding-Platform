## Core behaviour and test evidence

- The [14-case workbook](../../testdata/vendor_scenario_dataset.xlsx) contains four business types and a missing MSME proof, bank and tax mismatches, an unreadable cheque, a simulated registry timeout, a wrong document slot and rejection. All 14 observed rows match their prewritten expectations. V06–V08 test form checks and conditional checklists; they are **not** full submission and approval tests. V13 tests pre-submission completeness. V03 includes an additional two-step correction and approval sequence.
- [Scenario results](../../testdata/scenario_results.json) show the isolated bank conflict blocked at HTTP 400, the timeout blocked without a vendor reupload request, and wrong PAN-slot evidence prevented submission until replaced. The labelled evaluation set had **0 falsely approved known blockers** among the exercised blocking cases. This is not a production accuracy estimate.
- The backend regression suite finished **97 passed, 1 warning**. The current frontend build, lint and [browser checks](../../testdata/ui_results.json) passed; [fresh submission](../../grade_evidence/iteration2-fresh-submitted.png), [fresh approval](../../grade_evidence/iteration2-fresh-approved.png), [reviewer](../../grade_evidence/iteration2-reviewer.png), [correction](../../grade_evidence/iteration2-vendor-action.png) and [rejection](../../grade_evidence/iteration2-vendor-rejected.png) screenshots record the sampled views.

**Readiness gate:** passed for synthetic core behaviour. A fresh complete case reaches a recorded decision, bank conflict and provider outage follow different specified paths, and a rejected vendor sees the reason. The gate does not establish first-time usability or real registry accuracy. **Submission artifacts:** evaluator-accessible runnable link: no; a short silent recording under five minutes: yes. A narrated five-minute presentation has not been made.

## Highest-value remaining evidence

1. Walk a disputed field through the reviewer evidence screen, then complete a phone-sized correction through the vendor screen. The separate API recovery test proves the decision logic, but the full cross-role browser task remains unobserved.
2. Give a first-time vendor the correction and outage tasks without coaching; ask them to explain status and next owner. Record their actual words and actions before revising copy.
3. Run a scoped keyboard and contrast check on the vendor correction and reviewer evidence path. Several wizard inputs have visible text nearby but no programmatic label association.
4. Turn the short rehearsal into a paced, narrated five-minute demonstration and test what a nontechnical viewer remembers. Provide a runnable link and recording to an evaluator if submission requires them.
