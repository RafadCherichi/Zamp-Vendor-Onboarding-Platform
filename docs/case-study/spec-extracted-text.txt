1. Executive summary

The platform manages vendor onboarding from buyer invitation through business information collection, document submission, AI-assisted extraction, deterministic validation, external verification coordination, exception resolution and human approval. It is focused on vendor identity and onboarding, not the wider procurement lifecycle.

Product promiseCollect the right information once, check it consistently, explain what is wrong, make corrections easy, and give reviewers traceable evidence for decisions.

1.1 Problem

Vendor information is fragmented across forms, email and attachments.

Employees manually enter and compare PAN, GST, constitution and bank details.

Requirements vary by legal constitution, activity and buyer policy.

Vendors receive vague correction requests and repeat work.

External verification may be delayed, unavailable or restricted to authorised access.

Decisions and evidence are difficult to reconstruct without an audit trail.

1.2 Differentiation

Core differentiator: explainable exception resolution with change-aware resubmission. Identify the issue and evidence, tell the vendor what to correct, preserve prior versions, rerun dependent checks and give reviewers a clear comparison.

1.3 Product measures

Metric

Definition

First-pass completeness

Share of submissions with all buyer-required fields/documents.

Correction cycle time

Time from correction request to corrected resubmission.

Manual review rate

Share routed to review, segmented by exception type.

Rework rate

Checks repeated unnecessarily after a correction.

Provider availability

Verified, negative, unavailable and error rates reported separately.

Audit completeness

Decisions with actor, timestamp, reason and evidence.

Do not optimise only for automatic approval rate. Incorrectly passing uncertain submissions is worse than a justified review.

Phase

Decisions consolidated

2.1 Gaps found and resolved

Add asynchronous processing, persisted jobs, idempotency, bounded retries, correlation IDs and dead-letter handling.

Treat uploaded documents as untrusted input. Defend against prompt injection; never execute or obey instructions inside documents.

Add file security: MIME sniffing, extension allowlist, size/page limits, malware scanning where available, private storage and safe preview.

Separate application state, document state, validation status, exception status and provider status.

Provider timeout/unavailability must never be treated as verified or negative.

Same PAN alone must not automatically merge/reject: one legal identity may have multiple GST registrations/locations.

Udyam registration is permanent and does not require renewal according to the official portal; avoid generic expiry rules.

Proprietorship generally uses proprietor PAN; do not invent a separate proprietorship PAN.

A cancelled cheque proves printed details, not current account activity. Active verification requires an authorised provider.

OCR cannot establish authenticity by itself. Suspicious content is a review signal, not definitive fraud evidence.

Add retention/deletion, backup/restore, monitoring, incident response, provider contract review and production legal review.

Completeness conclusionEarlier phases covered the product workflow but under-specified operational architecture and security. This specification adds processing reliability, untrusted-file handling, provider resilience, data governance, audit integrity and entity-aware duplicate resolution.

3.1 Primary journey

Procurement creates expiring invitation with known vendor/contact context.

Vendor opens invitation, saves/resumes form, uploads required evidence and submits.

Backend stores original files and starts asynchronous processing.

Workers classify documents, extract fields, run deterministic rules and configured verification.

Exception manager routes vendor-correctable issues to vendor and ambiguous/high-risk issues to reviewers.

Vendor corrects fields or replaces affected evidence; revisions are retained and dependent checks rerun.

Reviewer examines evidence and records a reasoned decision.

Approved vendor identity and application snapshot are retained; later changes use controlled update workflow.

5.1 Separate status dimensions

Application lifecycle: overall workflow state.

Document status: uploaded, processing, readable, unreadable, wrong type, superseded.

Validation status: passed, failed, warning, unverified, unavailable, not applicable, pending.

Exception status: open, awaiting vendor, awaiting reviewer, retrying, resolved, waived with reason.

Verification status: not requested, pending, verified, negative, unavailable, provider error.

Only backend-authorised transitions change application state.

Approval blocked while buyer-policy-required blocking checks/exceptions remain.

Rejection requires reason; override/waiver requires actor, reason and evidence.

Corrections create revisions; previous values/files remain auditable.

Approval stores versioned snapshot of values, evidence references and validation outcomes.

Post-approval bank changes use controlled re-verification and approval.

6.1 Name matching

Class

Meaning

Action

Exact/normalised

Same after case, punctuation, spacing and approved suffix normalisation.

Pass; preserve original and normalised values.

Probable

Abbreviation, spelling variation or moderate similarity.

Human review; score is triage only.

Material mismatch

Substantially different identity/conflicting authoritative evidence.

Block approval; investigate.

Do not apply one global exact-match rule. A proprietor's personal PAN name may differ from trade name; companies may use suffix variations; bank names may be abbreviated. Retain original strings, normalisation algorithm/version and evidence.

6.2 Duplicate resolution

Strong signals: same CIN/LLPIN for same entity; same GSTIN; same PAN combined with corroborating identity fields.

Same PAN alone must not automatically merge/reject because a legal person may have multiple GST registrations/branches.

Same bank account is a risk signal, not conclusive duplicate proof.

Name/address/contact similarity is supporting evidence only.

Reviewer can link, keep separate or escalate; record decision.

11. AI and document-processing pipeline

Validate size, extension, detected MIME type and file safety.

Store original in private storage; calculate checksum; create document version.

Run OCR/layout extraction or vision extraction.

Request schema-constrained fields; unknown/null where evidence absent.

Run deterministic checks and cross-document matching.

Request configured external verification asynchronously.

Aggregate findings, create exceptions and update safe status.

11.1 AI guardrails

Treat document content as untrusted; ignore embedded instructions; never execute document content.

Require structured output and unknown rather than guessed values.

Capture field provenance where available.

LLM does not decide legal validity, bank ownership, fraud or final approval.

Confidence is extraction reliability, not vendor legitimacy.

Minimise personal data sent to AI; review provider retention, training, region and contract terms.

Record model/provider/version, prompt version and timestamp.

13. Security, privacy and audit

Separate vendor access from employee authentication; use high-entropy expiring/revocable invitation tokens; store token hashes where practical.

Apply role-based, organisation-scoped permissions on every endpoint; rate-limit invitation, login, upload and verification-trigger endpoints.

Private storage and short-lived signed URLs; allowlist PDF/JPEG/PNG initially; verify MIME; enforce size/page limits; scan/quarantine where available.

Safe preview rendering; immutable versions; retention/deletion policy for originals and derived extracts.

Encrypt in transit and at rest; secrets in managed secret store; never expose API keys in frontend, prompts or committed files.

Mask PAN/bank values in lists/logs; restrict access to documents/provider responses; audit sensitive access.

Clear privacy notice describing data, purposes, sharing and retention; minimise collection; review processor/provider terms.

Append-only audit events with actor, UTC timestamp, object, action, reason, evidence and correlation ID; restrict update/delete.

Monitor jobs/providers; support backup/restore, incident response and access revocation.

DPDP commencement noteThe DPDP Act and Rules were notified with phased commencement. The Gazette schedule specifies provisions commencing immediately, after one year and after eighteen months. Map each obligation to its commencement date and obtain legal review before production; do not claim every obligation is already operative.

This is a product/engineering specification, not a legal compliance certification.
